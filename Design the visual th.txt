Design the visual theme and the landing page for "Noctra", a night-time journey decision tool for night-shift workers (security guards, nurses, delivery riders, sanitation and factory workers, call-centre staff) in Indian cities. Tagline: "Navigate the Night. Get Home Safer."

The product: a worker finishes at 2:15 AM and must be home by 3:00 AM. Noctra shows feasible ways home, how much time is spare in the WORST case, a "decide by HH:MM" moment with a Plan B, and labels every fact as KNOWN, PROBABLE, OUTDATED or UNKNOWN instead of confidently guessing. It keeps working when the internet or live data disappears.

## TECH CONSTRAINTS
- React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui + lucide-react only. Plain React components.
- No Next.js features: no next/link, next/image, next/font, server components, app router, "use client". I am porting to Vite. Use <a> and <img>.
- All theme values as CSS variables in one globals.css, mapped in the Tailwind config. Structure the variables under :root (light, the default) so a .dark override block can be added later without touching components. Design ONLY the light theme now.
- One component per section in its own file (Navbar, Hero, ProblemSection, HowNoctraThinks, OfflineTiers, RealVsSimulated, FinalCta, Footer). No file over 200 lines. No animation libraries; CSS transitions only.

## ART DIRECTION: "daylight instrument panel"
A light, calm, precise interface. Think a well-made printed timetable crossed with a clean engineering tool. Trustworthy, not playful, not a startup template. Premium comes from restraint, alignment and generous space, not decoration.

Light tokens (verify WCAG AA for every text/background pair and tell me any pair that fails):

- text #14181D, muted #5B6672.
- Primary action: deep navy #12233A with white text (hover #1B3252). This is the ONLY filled button style.
- Amber #F2B54A is a FILL colour only (deadline bar, "decide by" marker, with #14181D text on it). For amber-coloured text use #8A5A00.

  - PROBABLE: text #1D5FA8 on tint #E8F0FA, half-filled circle.
  - OUTDATED: text #8A5A00 on tint #FBF1DC, clock icon, value struck through.
  - UNKNOWN: text #5E5780 on tint #EEECF5, dashed border, "?" icon.
- Shadows: one level only, 0 1px 2px rgba(20,24,29,.06), 0 8px 24px rgba(20,24,29,.06), used ONLY on the hero product preview and open dialogs. Everything else is separated by 1px borders.

## DASHBOARD SPACING SYSTEM (define as tokens now, used by the dashboard later)
Dashboards must breathe. Page padding 32px. Gap between modules 32px (24px on tablet). Card padding 24px (32px for hero modules). Gap between a card title and its content 16px; between list rows 12px with 56px minimum row height. Max 5 modules above the fold, max 3 list items per card plus a "View all" link, one primary button per card at most, never two competing accents in one card. Section titles 14px/600 muted, sentence case, no uppercase tracking. Show this system in a small "Spacing in practice" block on the design-tokens preview page.

## DO NOT (these read as AI-generated)
Gradient text, gradient backgrounds, glow, glassmorphism, blur, neon, floating blobs, grid-pattern or dot-pattern backgrounds, icon-in-a-colored-circle on cards, identical 3-column icon feature grids, rainbow badges, coloured borders on every card, emoji as icons, centered-everything layouts, buzzwords ("revolutionary", "seamless", "empower", "AI-powered", "next-gen"), stock photos, lorem ipsum, invented statistics, user counts, testimonials or logos.

## NAVBAR (exact)
Sticky, 64px high, solid #FFFFFF, 1px bottom border, no blur. Inner container max 1280px, centered, 24px padding. One flex row, three zones, everything vertically centered: logo left (wordmark "Noctra" with a small crescent-and-lamp mark drawn in SVG, no icon circle); links centre (How it works, Offline, Real vs Simulated; each 40px high, 12px horizontal padding, active = 2px navy bottom indicator); right: one primary button "Plan My Journey". No absolute positioning on items, no negative margins. Below 768px: logo + hamburger, links in a full-width sheet. Must not wrap or overflow at 1024, 768, 390.

## LANDING PAGE (build in this order)
1. HERO. Two columns (5/7 split), left-aligned. Headline (56px): "At 2:30 AM, the bus may or may not be running. Noctra tells you which." Supporting sentence (20px, muted): "Plan your way home with the worst case in view, a clear moment to switch plans, and an honest label on every fact." Primary button "Plan My Journey", secondary text link "See how it decides". Under the buttons, one muted line: "Built for CASCADE '26, IIIT Nagpur. Prototype with simulated data."
   Right column: a product preview card (white, 1px border, the single allowed shadow) containing, top to bottom: a header row (shift "Ends 2:15 AM", "Home by 3:00 AM", SIMULATED badge); a hand-built SVG map 320px high (muted warm-grey streets, a river or park shape, one navy route line, origin and destination markers, two small facility markers); then the NIGHT RIBBON: a horizontal timeline 2:15 to 3:00 with three nested arrival bands (best, expected, worst) in navy at three opacities, an amber "decide by 2:32" marker, and the status line "ON TIME, 14 min spare (worst case)"; then two EvidenceChips ("Road open: PROBABLE, 12 min ago" and "Cab availability: UNKNOWN"). Everything inside has 24px padding and 16px gaps; do not cram.
2. THE PROBLEM. Left column (5 cols) a 40px heading, "A city built for the daytime." Right column (6 cols, offset 1) two short paragraphs: cities are designed around people who work in the day, but millions of workers live on a different schedule; at 2:30 AM a worker may need food, medicine, transport or a safe route home, and existing apps treat night as just a different timestamp. No numbers. Below, "Who it is for": a 2-column definition list of six workers (security guard, nurse, delivery rider, sanitation worker, factory worker, call-centre agent), each with a one-line concrete need. Plain list with hairline dividers, no icons.
3. HOW NOCTRA THINKS. Heading "Every fact carries its age." Centrepiece, the AGE AXIS: a wide horizontal axis from "just now" to "hours ago" with an example fact ("Bus 14 running") and its chip changing along the axis: KNOWN (0 to 10 min) → OUTDATED (10 to 60 min) → UNKNOWN (beyond), with tick labels in mono. Show PROBABLE as a separate row for facts that come from incomplete evidence (e.g. a community report awaiting a second confirmation). Below the axis, four columns, one per label: the real EvidenceChip, a one-sentence definition, one example. Closing line: "Noctra never turns missing data into a confident answer." Flag the example times as illustrative.
4. WORKS WHEN THE NETWORK DOESN'T. Heading "Three tiers of degraded." Horizontal three-step diagram joined by a thin line with nodes, no cards-with-icons: Tier 0 "Live data" ; Tier 1 "Live feeds down: cached data, labels age visibly" ; Tier 2 "Server unreachable: last plan stays on the phone, emergency number 112 always shown". Under each, a tiny UI fragment (a banner in that tier's tone, drawn with real components).
5. REAL VS SIMULATED. Heading "What is real, and what is not." An honest table, 4 columns (Data, Source, Status, Note) with EvidenceChips or plain status text: roads and facilities (OpenStreetMap, cached); weather (cached when offline); risk ("Environmental risk proxy (not crime data)"); incidents and closures (simulated in demo); ride availability (unknown unless injected); transit schedules (only with a verified source, else unknown). Rows 56px high, hairline dividers, horizontally scrollable inside its own container on mobile.
6. FINAL CTA + FOOTER. A full-width raised (#F1EFE8) band: "Know your way home before you leave." + primary button. Footer on white: logo, three link groups, "© OpenStreetMap contributors", "CASCADE '26, IIIT Nagpur", note "Prototype. Data shown is simulated."

## COPY STYLE
Short, specific, engineer-careful sentences. Example: "At 2:30 AM the bus may or may not be running. Noctra tells you which, and how sure it is." Sentence case everywhere. No exclamation marks.

## ACCESSIBILITY
AA contrast on every pair, visible 2px navy focus ring with 2px offset, 48px touch targets on mobile, status never colour-only, prefers-reduced-motion respected, semantic landmarks and heading order.
